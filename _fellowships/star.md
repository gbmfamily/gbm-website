STAR — Singles Thriving and Reaching — is a fellowship for single adults in Grace Bible Mission. Singleness is not a waiting room; it is a season full of potential, purpose, and proximity to God. STAR exists to help single men and women thrive where they are, grow in faith, serve in ministry, and build community with one another.
