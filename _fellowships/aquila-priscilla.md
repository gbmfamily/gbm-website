Named after the faithful couple in Scripture, Aquila & Priscilla Fellowship is for young married couples in Grace Bible Mission. Marriage is a covenant and a calling — and this fellowship exists to help couples grow together in Christ, build godly homes, and minister as one. Here, couples find community, accountability, and practical discipleship for the journey of marriage.
