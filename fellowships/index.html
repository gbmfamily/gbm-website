A dedicated fellowship for teenagers in Grace Bible Mission, raising the next generation in faith, purpose and the Word of God.

Students Fellowship

For polytechnic, university and higher institution students. A community where students grow in faith and navigate academics with God.

A fellowship for women of all ages in Grace Bible Mission — a space for prayer, the Word and sisterhood in Christ.
