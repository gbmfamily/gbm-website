Men of Vision is a fellowship for men of Grace Bible Mission — a gathering where men are built in the Word, sharpened in character, and strengthened in vision. Leadership starts at home, extends to the church, and reaches into the community. Men of Vision exists to raise men who lead well in every arena God has placed them.
