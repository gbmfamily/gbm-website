Bride of the Lord is a fellowship for widows and widowers in Grace Bible Mission. Grief is real, but so is the resurrection hope we carry. This fellowship is a place of genuine comfort, mutual strength, and renewed purpose — where those who have lost a spouse find that they are never alone, because Christ Himself is close to the brokenhearted.
